Double It is a puzzle game. It has no accounts or sign-in, and it does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos
or location.

## What stays on your phone

The game saves your settings (sound, vibration, theme), your best scores, your breezes, the run in progress and simple
play statistics. It uses those statistics to adjust the pace of the game to how you play. These are stored only on your
device. If Android backup is on, Android may copy them to your own Google account backup so they come back after a
reinstall. We (the developer) never receive this data.

## Ads (optional, off unless you turn them on)

Ads are **off by default**. If you turn them on in **Pause > Support**, the game shows a small banner from
**Google AdMob**, only on menus and the game-over screen, never while you play.

- Before any ad is requested, Google's consent tool (the **User Messaging Platform**) asks for your consent where the
  law requires it, for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland. You can change your choice at any time with
  **Pause > Support > privacy choices**.
- When ads are on, Google may collect and use information such as your device's **advertising ID**, IP address
  (approximate location), device and app information, and how you interact with ads, to show ads, measure them and
  prevent fraud. Whether ads are personalised depends on your consent and your device settings.
- You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (**Settings > Privacy > Ads**).
- Turning ads off again in **Pause > Support** stops the banner.

How Google uses this information: <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>

## Purchases

Breeze packs are bought through **Google Play**. Google handles the payment; we never see your card or payment details.
We only learn that a purchase succeeded so the game can add the breezes.

## Permissions

- **Vibration**: for gentle haptic feedback (you can turn it off in Pause).
- **Internet** and **advertising ID**: used only by Google's ad and consent tools, and only when ads are turned on.

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
